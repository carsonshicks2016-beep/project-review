## Raw control-volume momentum audit (2026-10-02)

The pinned IBAMR 0.19.0 source confirms that its moving-control-volume force formula includes `−(P_box,new−P_box,current)/dt`; the app also calls `computeLaggedMomentumIntegral()` before the advance, as required by the API. Therefore the startup spike is not explained by a missing initialization call. A fresh instrumented single-level run recorded the raw terms and fixed box bounds. For each half-domain box, analytic momentum for the exact uniform state is `0.177401167373 kg m/s`. IBAMR's new-state integral is `0.177401167371 kg m/s` (relative error `−1.1e-9%`), while its lagged current-state integral is `0.174629274131 kg m/s` (`−1.5625%`). The resulting spurious-looking box momentum rate is `+592.662 N` per box and the reported summed force is `−1,185.324 N`; independent pressure/advection/viscous surface flux is approximately `−0.00485 N` total.

This is direct evidence that the anomalous force comes from a mismatch in the old/new control-volume momentum terms, despite unchanged reported Eulerian flow and fixed bounds. The particular cause of the `1.5625%` lagged-integral error is still unresolved; likely areas for the next test are the actual staggered face data used by `fillPatchData()`, patch-layout/volume weights across the initial step, and whether the pre-advance integral samples precisely the same composite hierarchy representation as the post-advance integral. Do not classify the force as physical or Stage A as repaired. The artifact is `results/stage_a_force_momentum_terms/force_isolation_summary.json`; Stage A remains **FAILED / UNVALIDATED**.
