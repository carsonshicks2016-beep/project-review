============================================================
  BLADE — curriculum trainer · loadouts · live 3D viewer
============================================================

LOADOUTS:  none  sword  shield  sword_shield  mace  axe  katana

------------------------------------------------------------
TRAIN one fighter through the curriculum (STAND→WALK→TERRAIN→SPAR).
Headless, with the lab-grade dashboard; auto-promotes between stages.

    python3 -m blade.train --name knight  --loadout sword_shield
    python3 -m blade.train --name reaper  --loadout axe
    python3 -m blade.train --name ronin   --loadout katana
    python3 -m blade.train --name brawler --loadout none

  continue a run (same stage it left off on):
    python3 -m blade.train --name knight --resume

  flags: --iters 20000  --envs 24  --rollout 64  --ent 0.003
         --eval-every 15  --workers 14  --seed N

------------------------------------------------------------
WATCH live in clean 3D (simplified viewer, no mjpython needed).
Hot-reloads as it trains and follows the curriculum into new stages.

    python3 -m blade.view --name knight
    python3 -m blade.view --name knight --stage SPAR     # force the combat world
    python3 -m blade.view --name reaper --stage STAND    # watch any stage

------------------------------------------------------------
MATCHMAKER — pit any two trained fighters (any loadouts, even mixed).

    python3 -m blade.arena --left knight --right reaper          # sword+shield vs axe -> mp4
    python3 -m blade.arena --left knight --right knight          # mirror
    python3 -m blade.arena --left knight --right reaper --live   # clean 3D window
