UNDER THE HOOD

Learning, one lap at a time.

48 neural drivers share a procedurally generated gravel circuit. Each driver sees 12 inputs: upcoming road direction, sideways offset, speed, sliding, curvature, slope, tire load, previous steering and a bias.

Two neural outputs control steering and throttle / brake. After each generation, the best drivers survive; their weights are combined and mutated to create the next population.

Finish first. Then go faster. Completing a lap always beats an incomplete attempt. Once drivers finish, shorter lap times earn higher scores.

This lightweight simulation includes tire grip, sliding, drag, road gradient and elevation-dependent tire load. It uses a noisy road-following starting policy, not a pretrained driver. It is an educational model, not a full vehicle dynamics simulator.

New tracks retain the best policy to test transfer. Export saves its weights and track settings. Training runs entirely in this tab; closing it ends the session.
