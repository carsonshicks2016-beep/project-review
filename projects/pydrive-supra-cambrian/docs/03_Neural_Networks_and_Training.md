# Neural Networks & Training

Supra Drift utilizes two independent AI training pathways to master its physics engine: a Genetic Algorithm (GA) and a PyTorch Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) reinforcement learning path. Both paths use the exact same observation space to perceive the world.

## Observation Space (The Senses)

The agent sees a 60-dimensional vector composed of the following blocks:

- **Vision (9)**: Raycast beams (±100°, 70 m range) mapping distance to the track walls.
- **Proprioception (25)**: Body velocities, yaw rate, g-forces, chassis slip, per-wheel load, grip-usage, slip ratio, RPM, gear, boost, steering angle, and track-relative position.
- **Look-ahead Curvature (6)**: Signed centreline curvature at 6 distances down the road (8–90 m).
- **Hills & Air (18)**: Road grade and bank under the body, actual pitch, vertical speed, height above the road, an airborne flag, plus look-ahead grade AND look-ahead crest curvature at the same 6 distances. (With its own speed, the network can predict a takeoff before it happens and time the landing).
- **Mode Flag (2)**: A one-hot vector indicating the active mode (`[race, drift]`), which allows for goal-conditioned hybrid training.

## The Genetic Algorithm (GA) Path

The GA evolves a population of NumPy-based Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) "brains" to drive the track. 

- **Fitness Metric**: Centreline distance covered.
- **Evolution Mechanisms**: Elitism (the saved champion is always the best ever seen), tournament selection, uniform crossover, and Gaussian mutation. 
- **Keep-Best**: GA has inherent keep-best via elitism, so training longer is never worse.

```bash
# Train a specialist GA on Akina
python3 run.py --train 200 --track akina --out akina_ga.npz 

# Watch the swarm evolve live in the viewer
python3 run.py --train 100 --live 
```

## The PPO Reinforcement Learning Path (Race & Drift)

The primary path is a PyTorch actor-critic PPO setup featuring Generalized Advantage Estimation (GAE), a clipped surrogate objective, value/entropy losses, in-process vectorized environments, and a difficulty curriculum.

The actor network includes a forward-throttle init bias (so the untrained policy moves rather than stands still), and `log_std` is clamped to `[-2.2, 0]` to prevent entropy from exploding in long runs.

### PPO Race
Focuses on pure lap time and progress.
```bash
python3 run.py --ppo 600                                   # Generalist (curriculum)
python3 run.py --ppo 800 --track akina --out akina_race.pt # Specialist
```

### PPO Drift
Focuses on drifting through corners.
- **Reward Function**: `Slip-angle × Speed`.
- **Anti-Spin Shaping**: Uses a yaw-rate gate to scale down rewards if the car spins out of control. It also includes a controlled-hold bonus for keeping a steady angle through a corner.
- **Curriculum Gates**: A completion-gated curriculum ensures the agent can actually finish a lap before moving to a harder track. It prevents the AI from learning to just "spin out for points".

```bash
python3 run.py --drift 1500                                # Generalist drift
python3 run.py --drift 2000 --track national --out national_drift.pt
```

### PPO Hybrid
A fused policy that races the straights for speed and drifts the corners for style. Uses the race backbone (progress/speed) but adds a style bonus gated to corners.

```bash
python3 run.py --hybrid 1500 --style 0.8
```

## Deterministic Evaluation

During training, rolling numbers overstate performance due to exploration noise. The true metric is the **`[eval]`** line, generated by a deterministic, full-episode run (`act_mean`, no noise) from fixed starting points. This is exactly what you see when you watch a saved checkpoint. `_best.pt` saves and early-stopping mechanisms key entirely off of this deterministic evaluation, never the rolling training numbers.
