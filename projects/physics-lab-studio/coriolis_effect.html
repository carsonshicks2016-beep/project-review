Fictitious Force

The Coriolis effect isn't a real push or pull. It's an apparent deflection caused by observing motion from a rotating frame of reference (like Earth). In an inertial frame (space), objects move straight!

Hurricane Rotation

Air rushes toward low pressure. In the Northern Hemisphere, it's deflected to the right, causing a counter-clockwise spin. In the South, it deflects left, causing a clockwise spin.

The Equator Rule

At the equator (φ = 0°), the Coriolis parameter f = 2Ω sin(0) = 0. Without deflection, air flows straight into a low-pressure center, filling it before a hurricane can form. Hurricanes need |φ| > 5°.
