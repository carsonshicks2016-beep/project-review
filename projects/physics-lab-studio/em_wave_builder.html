1. Origin of Waves

Electromagnetic waves are generated by accelerating electric charges. When a charge oscillates, it creates a changing electric field, which induces a changing magnetic field, and so on. This self-sustaining disturbance propagates through space at the speed of light.

2. Orthogonal Fields

Maxwell's equations dictate that the Electric (E) and Magnetic (B) fields must be mutually perpendicular, and both are perpendicular to the direction of wave propagation (transverse wave). They oscillate exactly in phase in a vacuum.

3. Polarization in Tech

Polarization describes the orientation of the E-field. Polarized sunglasses block horizontally polarized glare. 3D cinema glasses use circular polarization (one lens Right, one Left) so tilting your head doesn't ruin the 3D effect!
