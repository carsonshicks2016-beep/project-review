1. Tilting Horizontal Roll

Wind shear (different wind speeds at different altitudes) creates invisible horizontal rolling tubes of air. A strong convective updraft catches this roll and tilts it vertically into a rotating mesocyclone.

2. Conservation of Angular Momentum

As the updraft stretches the vortex tube vertically, its radius shrinks. Like an ice skater pulling in their arms, v × r = constant forces the rotational wind speeds (v_θ) to increase dramatically.

3. The Condensation Funnel

Cyclostrophic balance causes a massive pressure drop in the core. This expands and cools the air adiabatically. If it cools below the dew point, water vapor condenses, forming the visible funnel cloud.
