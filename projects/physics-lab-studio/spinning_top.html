1. Why doesn't it fall?

Gravity exerts a downward force on the top's center of mass, creating a Torque (τ) about the tip. Instead of pulling the top down, torque changes the direction of the angular momentum L. Since τ is perpendicular to L, the top sweeps out a horizontal circle (dL/dt = τ).

2. Inverse Relationship

Precession speed Ωp = (m·g·d) / (I·ωs). Notice that spin speed ωs is in the denominator! Spinning the top FASTER makes it precess SLOWER. As friction slows the top down, precession speeds up until it becomes unstable and topples.

3. The Sleeping Top

If spun fast enough perfectly vertically (θ=0), frictional torques pull the top perfectly upright. It requires a critical spin speed ωcrit to maintain this state. This gyroscopic stability is used in spacecraft navigation, airplanes, and smartphones.
