🧲 Gauss's Law & Charges

Inside a conductor in electrostatic equilibrium, the net electric field is zero. By Gauss's Law (∮ E · dA = Q_enclosed / ε0), this means the enclosed charge must be zero. Any excess charge resides entirely on the outer surface, redistributing instantly to cancel external fields.

✈️ Lightning Safety

Airplanes and cars act as partial Faraday cages. When struck by lightning (millions of volts), the current travels along the conductive exterior skin to the ground or air, leaving the interior and its passengers perfectly safe with zero internal electric field.

📻 Aperture Cutoff (Meshes)

A Faraday cage doesn't need to be solid metal! A wire mesh works if the holes are significantly smaller than the wavelength of the electromagnetic wave. This is why a microwave oven door has a mesh: it blocks ~12cm microwaves but lets ~500nm visible light pass through.
