The Reynolds Number (Re)

Defined as Re = ρvD/μ, it's the ratio of inertial forces (momentum keeping fluid moving) to viscous forces (friction keeping fluid together).

At low Re, honey-like viscosity dominates (smooth flow). At high Re, inertia dominates, leading to instability and chaos.

Hopf Bifurcation & Shedding

Around Re ≈ 47, the symmetric eddies behind the cylinder become unstable. A tiny perturbation causes them to peel off alternately.

This forms the von Kármán Vortex Street. The shedding frequency f = St·v/D depends on the Strouhal number (St ≈ 0.2).

Real-World Impacts

This oscillating wake creates alternating low-pressure zones, pulling the object side to side. It causes telephone wires to "sing" in the wind, car antennas to vibrate, and famously contributed to the twisting collapse of the Tacoma Narrows Bridge.
