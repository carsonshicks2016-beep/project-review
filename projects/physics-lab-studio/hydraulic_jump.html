1. The Sonic Boom Analogy

A hydraulic jump is the exact fluid equivalent of a supersonic shock wave. When flow speed exceeds the speed of surface gravity waves (Fr > 1), information cannot travel upstream. The fluid "crashes" into the slower downstream water, forcing a sudden, turbulent increase in depth to balance momentum.

2. Froude Number (Fr)

Fr = v / √(g·y)
It compares flow inertia (velocity v) to gravitational restoring forces (wave speed √(g·y)).
• Fr > 1: Supercritical (fast/thin).
• Fr < 1: Subcritical (slow/deep).

3. Civil Engineering

Dam spillways use stilling basins to intentionally trigger a hydraulic jump. The intense turbulence acts as a massive brake, dissipating huge amounts of destructive kinetic energy (ΔE) so the water doesn't scour and destroy the downstream riverbed.
