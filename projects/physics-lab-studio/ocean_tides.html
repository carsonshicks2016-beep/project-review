1. The Two Bulges Paradox

Why are there two high tides? Gravity pulls the near-side water harder than Earth's center, creating the front bulge. Earth's center is pulled harder than the far-side water, effectively pulling Earth away from the far ocean, creating the second, rear bulge!

2. The Inverse-Cube Law (1/r³)

Tidal forces depend on the difference in gravity across a body, not absolute gravity. This differential force drops off as 1/r³. Thus, the Moon's tidal pull is ~2.2x stronger than the Sun's, even though the Sun is 27M times more massive!

3. Spring vs Neap Tides

When the Sun, Earth, and Moon align (New/Full Moon), their tidal bulges add constructively to create extreme Spring Tides. When at 90° (Quarter Moon), the Sun's bulge partially cancels the Moon's, creating weak Neap Tides.
