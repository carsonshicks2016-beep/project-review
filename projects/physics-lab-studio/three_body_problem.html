The Butterfly Effect

Unlike the 2-body problem (Earth & Sun), the 3-body problem has no general analytic solution. In 1890, Henri Poincaré proved that trajectories exhibit deterministic chaos. A microscopic 0.0001% change in starting positions leads to exponentially diverging futures—the birth of chaos theory.

Choreographies

Despite the chaos, rare stable periodic solutions exist. The famous "Figure-8" orbit (discovered in 1993) has three equal masses chasing each other eternally on an infinity curve. Euler and Lagrange also found stable collinear and equilateral triangle configurations in the 18th century.

Ejections & Slingshots

Most random 3-body systems end violently. Through complex energy exchanges, two bodies will form a tight binary pair, acting as a gravitational slingshot that ejects the third body into deep space at high velocity. This is how rogue planets and hypervelocity stars are flung from galaxies!
