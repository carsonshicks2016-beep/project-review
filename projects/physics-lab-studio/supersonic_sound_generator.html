1. Subsonic (M < 1)

The sound waves travel at 343 m/s, faster than the aircraft. As the jet flies, waves compress ahead (Doppler blueshift / higher pitch) and stretch behind (redshift / lower rumble). The listener hears the approaching jet well before it passes overhead.
