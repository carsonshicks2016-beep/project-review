Why Pitch Changes

As the vehicle moves forward, it "chases" the sound waves it just emitted. This compresses the wavefronts ahead (higher frequency/pitch) and stretches them behind (lower frequency/pitch). This is known as the Doppler Effect.

The Mathematics

f_obs = f_src / (1 - (v/c) * cos(θ)) Where c is the speed of sound (343 m/s), v is vehicle speed, and θ is the angle between the velocity and the observer.

Real-World Sirens

Emergency sirens alternate between high and low tones. The sudden drop in pitch as they pass helps pedestrians localize the vehicle's direction and realize it has passed, reducing confusion in urban environments.
