MODEL CONTRACT

What is being simulated

A deterministic, whole-link flow approximation on an OSM-derived directed network. Sending flow is bounded by capacity and available vehicles; shared receiving budgets bound merges and link storage.

Fixed-time control is compared with scheduled contraflow, rule-based signal coordination, perimeter routing and incident response. No learned policy or training checkpoint is present.

Vehicle conservation

Initial + generated = exited + on-road + boundary queue

Conservation residuals are checked during every step. Rejected arrivals wait at the network boundary; they are never silently discarded.

Metric definitions

Network TSTT integrates modeled on-road occupancy. System time adds external queue waiting. Speed-based delay and fuel calculations remain heuristic proxies.

VALIDITY BOUNDARY

Before a real-world claim

  1. Calibrate demand and capacity against observed counts, speeds and queues.
  2. Connect destination-specific routes, parking occupancy and parked-vehicle egress.
  3. Replace street-name signal matching with actual intersection movements.
  4. Establish timestep convergence and compare with a reference traffic simulator.
  5. Validate on held-out game days and report sensitivity to uncertain inputs.

The current experiments are exploratory. No confidence interval, capacity gain or operational recommendation should be inferred from the prescribed legacy curves.
