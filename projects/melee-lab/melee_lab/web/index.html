Policy architecture

CURRENT CHECKPOINT CONTRACT

The bot reads internal game state with no added observation delay. CPU-9 success alone does not establish human-level or superhuman play. A replay-imitation anchor, a historical opponent league, and a promotion gauntlet exist but have not yet been shown to improve play. Recurrent memory, broader stages, and controlled human evaluation remain substantial work.

Controller & move laboratory

Input / move contextFramesCommitmentMain stickButtons

These are controller inputs, not guaranteed successful techniques. The same input produces different moves on the ground, in the air, during grabs, or in hitstun. Full game-state access and no added delay differ from human perception. One-frame decisions allow 60 input decisions per game second; move animations still obey Melee’s engine.

Research direction: expert replay imitation followed by reinforcement learning and controlled human evaluation. See Slippi AI ↗ and action-delay research ↗.

    Starts a new policy with the factored controller: every stick angle, C-stick, button combo and shield depth is reachable. Creates a fresh, unevaluated policy.
    Trains with an internal LSTM recurrent hidden state across frames rather than an isolated 2-frame window. Tracks opponent conditioning, tech habits, and play patterns across the match.
    Keeps cloning the demonstrations while PPO plays, at a weight that decays to zero across the budget. Lowers the learning rate and entropy bonus so PPO does not wash the clone out before its value head calibrates.
    Episode count applies to each of six matchups. Every matchup is reported separately.
    Checkpoints save every 2,048 decisions. A stop saves progress before closing emulators. Demonstration wins never count as agent wins.
